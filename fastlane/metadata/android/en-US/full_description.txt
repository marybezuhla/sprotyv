A free civil-protection app for Ukraine: what to do right now when it is dangerous, and how to prepare in advance. Works without internet. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

<b>What is there</b>
<ul>
<li><b>What to do now:</b> step-by-step cards for air alerts, bleeding, choking, drones, fire, rubble and more. The screen stays on while your hands are busy.</li>
<li><b>A guide</b> to survival and basic first aid, the Ministry of Health first-aid procedures, the Constitution and the Rules of the Road in full (the last three in Ukrainian).</li>
<li><b>Daily life in a crisis:</b> no power, heat, water, gas or connection.</li>
<li><b>I am safe:</b> a ready message to your family via WhatsApp, Signal or SMS.</li>
<li><b>My data:</b> medical card, family plan, trusted people, go-bag, supplies, notes. All encrypted and kept only on the phone; a backup with a password.</li>
<li><b>Readiness:</b> roles, a readiness path, daily resilience, shields and counts of good deeds; where to learn, including the regional centres for national resistance.</li>
<li><b>Widgets,</b> simple and full looks, a kids' mode, trip mode and a neutral icon.</li>
<li><b>112</b> on every screen; a tap opens the dialler.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
The app has no internet permission. Personal data is kept in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) with its key in the phone's secure keystore. Android cloud backup is off.

It does not replace a doctor, rescuers, official instructions or sirens.
